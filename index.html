About M.I. VII

M.I. VIII Global Security Consulting is a mission-driven geopolitical advisory and education firm bridging security and sustainable development. We serve government agencies, multinational corporations, universities, and NGOs with solutions combining strategic insight, education, and talent development.

Our Vision

Education is the cornerstone of lasting security. By uniting intelligence, innovation, and inclusivity, we build networks that strengthen institutions and promote a secure, sustainable future.

Our Approach

We combine national security analysis rigor with workforce development accessibility. Meeting clients where they are, we design scalable strategies that cultivate the next generation of global leaders.

Advisory Board

Our advisors bring decades of experience across defense, intelligence, and international development sectors.

Myles Ibanez

Founder

Myles Ibanez is a geopolitical risk advisor and national security strategist with extensive experience supporting U.S. defense posture and international engagement. As Founder and CEO of M.I. VIII Global Security Consulting LLC, he bridges security and sustainable development through strategic fellowships, consulting, and global partnerships. His work spans AFRICOM, INDOPACOM, EUCOM, and CENTCOM, supporting threat reduction, operational frameworks, and regional security advisory. With over a decade developing global programs—including roles with the U.S. Marine Corps, Defense Threat Reduction Agency, and Octant Associates—Myles advances strategic talent development and applied international security training.

Adom Cooper

National Security & Foreign Policy Advisor

Adom M. Cooper is a national security and foreign policy professional, lawyer, and writer specializing in risk management, policy development, and emerging technologies. He has served the U.S. government, United Nations, American Society of International Law, and private consulting firms. Cooper holds a JD from Syracuse University College of Law and a BA from the University of Michigan. He authored the children's cybersecurity book RhinoRAM, is a Term Member at Council on Foreign Relations, and serves on the DC Bar. An avid traveler, he has walked on all seven continents.

Dr. Christopher Shell

Advisor

Dr. Christopher Shell is a historian and geopolitical analyst specializing in domestic drivers of U.S. foreign policy and Caribbean engagement. Based at a Washington, D.C. think tank, he provides research-driven strategic policy analysis and regional diplomacy insights. His work appears in Foreign Policy, World Politics Review, The National Interest, and Responsible Statecraft. Dr. Shell holds a Ph.D. in History from Michigan State University and advises at the intersection of domestic politics and international strategy.

Dr. Letisha Malcolm

International Education Advisor

Dr. Letisha Malcolm is an educator, strategist, and international affairs expert with over 15 years of experience across K-12 and higher education, the United Nations, UNESCO, and intergovernmental organizations. A Fulbright Scholar, she holds dual doctoral degrees in Education Administration and Social Policy from Morgan State University and a certificate in U.S. Public Policy from Harvard Extension School. Dr. Malcolm leads global initiatives in leadership development, policy innovation, and international collaboration. Her expertise spans strategic planning, policy analysis, and public diplomacy, focusing on inclusive governance, economic empowerment, and youth leadership. She has delivered keynote addresses at the UN General Assembly, U.S. State Department, and other prestigious venues.

Colonel (Ret) Mark Shuster

Advisor at large

Mark Shuster is a senior operations and program management executive with over 33 years leading defense and aerospace initiatives. A retired U.S. Marine Corps Colonel, he brings expertise in military logistics, government contracting, systems integration, and strategic program execution. As Corporate Director at Delta Solutions & Strategies, Mr. Shuster oversees defense contracts exceeding $62 million supporting the U.S. Air Force and Navy. His leadership spans mission-critical operations, ISO-certified quality systems, and lifecycle program management. His military career includes directing infantry combat training for Marines deploying to Iraq and Afghanistan. He holds a B.A. from California State University, Northridge, and is a graduate of the U.S. Air War College and USMC Command and Staff College.

8 Foundational Pillars

M.I. VIII integrates strategy and purpose through Mission Integration, guided by eight foundational pillars that demonstrate our commitment to transforming ideas into impact.

Strategic Security Integration

We approach security as an interconnected system—one that demands coordination across defense, diplomacy, innovation, and education. Our model ensures that every strategic effort reinforces the next, breaking down silos between government, academia, and industry. By aligning these forces, we develop frameworks that respond to global threats with precision and purpose. Integration isn’t just about efficiency; it’s about creating cohesive, sustainable solutions that strengthen institutions, empower communities, and advance collective stability.

Intelligence & Risk Analysis

We believe that informed action begins with disciplined intelligence. Through the responsible use of open-source and commercial data, we identify patterns, map risk environments, and anticipate disruption before it occurs. Our research-driven approach transforms raw information into strategic foresight—supporting leaders, policymakers, and organizations as they navigate an increasingly complex world. By grounding every recommendation in rigorous analysis, we provide clarity in uncertainty and empower decision-makers to act confidently, ethically, and with measurable impact.

Education & Workforce Development

We see diplomacy as the foundation of sustainable security. Building genuine partnerships across nations, sectors, and cultures allows us to bridge divides and amplify shared goals. Our collaborations are rooted in respect, transparency, and a belief that global stability requires cooperation, not competition. By fostering networks that connect governments, institutions, and innovators, we help turn dialogue into action—strengthening the bonds that protect peace, advance prosperity, and uphold the principles of shared responsibility in an interdependent world.

Global Partnerships & Diplomacy

We close the gap between academic learning and real-world readiness. Our fellowships, leadership programs, and skills-based training prepare individuals to meet the challenges of modern security and policy work. By empowering emerging professionals with the tools, mentorship, and networks they need, we build a workforce capable of advancing innovation and solving global challenges. Education, to us, is not only about knowledge—it’s about equipping people to lead, to serve, and to shape a more resilient and equitable future.

Innovation & Emerging Technology

We view technology as both a tool and a responsibility. By integrating artificial intelligence, data analytics, and digital platforms into our operations, we enhance our capacity to understand, anticipate, and respond to global challenges. Innovation drives our ability to adapt and create new pathways for collaboration across borders. We don’t innovate for novelty’s sake—we innovate to make systems smarter, policies more informed, and communities more secure in an era defined by rapid technological change.

Leadership & Career Advancement

We develop leaders who think critically, act ethically, and adapt fearlessly. Our leadership philosophy is rooted in mentorship, reflection, and collaboration. We prepare individuals to navigate uncertainty, inspire trust, and influence outcomes in complex global systems. Through immersive programs and guided growth, we help professionals discover their purpose and strengthen their ability to lead with integrity. Leadership, for us, is not a title—it’s a commitment to driving progress through courage, competence, and compassion.

Sustainable Development & Resilience

We recognize that true security depends on sustainability. Our work integrates environmental stewardship, economic opportunity, and social equity into security frameworks to build long-term resilience. By empowering communities to thrive in the face of geopolitical and ecological disruption, we strengthen the foundation for global stability. Sustainability is not a separate objective—it’s embedded in how we design systems, deploy solutions, and measure success. Resilience is the product of balance—between people, planet, and progress.

Ethics & Accountability

We operate with integrity at every level of engagement. Accountability is the thread that connects our values to our impact. Every partnership, program, and policy we undertake is guided by transparency, fairness, and respect for those we serve. We believe that ethical governance is not optional—it is essential to trust, credibility, and lasting influence. By holding ourselves accountable to the highest standards, we ensure that our mission advances both national security and human dignity.
