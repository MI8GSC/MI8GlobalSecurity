M.I.VIII Vanguard Fellowship

Developing young global leaders through research, mentorship, and real-world experience in international affairs and food security.

Apply Now

Program Overview

The M.I.VIII Vanguard Fellowship is a 6-month leadership development experience for young men (ages 16-21), combining hands-on research, expert mentorship, and global policy education. Fellows work in small teams to investigate real-world global challenges through monthly seminars, collaborative projects, and a final capstone presentation.

April - August

Six-month intensive program with monthly in-person and virtual sessions.

Hybrid Location

Monthly seminars at Georgetown University (Washington, D.C.), with virtual OSINT trainings online.

Global Focus Areas

Food Security, Governance, Migration, Climate Change, Public Health, Conflict & Security.

Capstone Project

Fellows present research findings to a panel of subject-matter experts at the program's conclusion.

Program Highlights

University Seminars

Monthly interactive lectures led by Georgetown faculty.

Expert Briefings

Podcast-style conversations with leaders in policy, security, and business.

OSINT Training

Hands-on open-source intelligence research sessions.

Project Presentation

Participants present research to expert panels for publication readiness.

Leadership Rotation

Each student rotates through team leadership and specialized roles.

Optional Outings

Networking and community-building events throughout the program.

Expectations & Conduct

  • Mandatory attendance for all scheduled sessions
  • Professional dress code (collared shirt minimum)
  • Respectful, collaborative behavior at all times
  • Leadership roles rotate monthly within each team
  • WhatsApp group communication for accountability
  • All concerns must be promptly reported to program managers
